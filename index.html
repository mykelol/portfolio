Projects

Engineering & coding work with demo, writeup, and concept images.

PROJECT 01

Trash Collecting RC Boat

MECHANICAL DESIGN SOLIDWORKS EMBEDDED SYSTEMS
  • Designed and 3D-printed a remote-controlled catamaran to clear debris off the surfaces of lakes and ponds to make small-scale water pollution cleanup more efficient and less labor-intensive.
  • Modeled the chassis and propellers via SolidWorks and 3D-printed the parts using Bambu Studio software and printers.
  • Programmed an ESP32 microcontroller in C++ using Arduino IDE to control two TT motors and integrated wireless smartphone control using Blynk IoT.
  • Iterated electronics enclosures and hull design to minimize heat warping and optimize buoyancy after field testing in local pond environments.
PROJECT 02

Multiplayer Stimulation Clicker

PYTHON PYGAME GAME DEVELOPMENT
  • Developed a remake of the online clicker game "Stimulation Clicker" in Python utilizing Pygame while adding multiplayer functionality, original minigames, unique upgrades, and new challenges to enhance the game's complexity and competitiveness.
  • Established a game state system to manage transitions between menu, gameplay, shop, scoreboard, and pause states
  • Created custom classes for individual game assets to easily handle animation and player interaction.
  • Balanced upgrade costs and minigame difficulty through multiple rounds of playtesting to keep progression rewarding and engaging.
PROJECT 03

Life-Sized Trebuchet

APPLIED MECHANICS WOODWORKING
  • Engineered and built a 6-foot tall trebuchet capable of launching a two pound projectile over 30 meters, demonstrating principles of torque and energy transfer
  • Simulated different arm lengths and projectile weights through VirtualTrebuchet to maximize launch distance given a 50-pound counterweight constraint.
  • Iterated through different release mechanisms and sling pouches after initial testing showed irregular launch angles
